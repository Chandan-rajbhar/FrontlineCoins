FRONTLINE COINS

Custom Challenge Coins & Insignia App

Proposed Digital Experience

The proposed Frontline Coins USA mobile web application enhances law enforcement, military, and agency insignia procurement operations through:

Instant 4-Step Quote Builder & Real-Time Pricing Intake

Agency procurement officers and unit commanders can configure customized coin production runs by selecting diameter specifications (1.50" compact to 2.25" heavyweight), quantity tiers (50 to 500+ pieces), and premium metallic finishes (Antique Brass, Polished Gold, Antique Silver, and Dual Plating)—complete with real-time price estimation, transparent per-unit rates, and automatic zero mold fee eligibility.

Categorized Multi-Product Insignia Catalog & Filtering

Users can explore and filter professional specification-grade offerings across Challenge Coins, Lapel Pins, Law Enforcement Badges, Medals, Key Chains, Bottle Openers, Trail / Vehicle Badges, and Poker Chips with upfront tiered pricing, certified quality guarantees, high-relief 3D photography, and detailed metal core specifications.

Live 7-Stage Production Timeline & Order Tracking

Officers can monitor custom orders in real time across the full manufacturing lifecycle—from Quote and 3D Artwork Approval to Die Stamping, Hand-Inspection Quality Check, Defense Courier Dispatch, and Direct Station Delivery—with active step indicators, tracking references, and estimated delivery dates.

3D Artwork Proof Desk & Direct Dispatch Support

Clients can review photorealistic digital proof renderings, compare multi-version revision histories (v1.0 to v3.0 Active), collaborate directly with insignia designers through real-time feedback notes, and access one-tap 24/7 direct phone and email assistance operated by veteran peace officers.

Together, these mobile features simplify custom coin manufacturing, streamline agency procurement without mold fees, and provide active-duty units with complete production transparency from concept to delivery.

Core App Modules Included:

Catalog & Configuration: Discovery Feed, Category Chips, Filterable Shop Catalog, Specification-Grade Product Detail, Interactive Quote Builder & Tier Estimator
Order Lifecycle & Support: Active Order Timeline Dashboard, 3D Proof Approval Desk, Officer Profile & Saved Armory Designs, 24/7 Operations Support & FAQs
